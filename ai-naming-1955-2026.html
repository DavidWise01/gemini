Evidence standard

Semantic meaning of a term is not an original selection rationale. Substantive rationale connects the precise name to a documented design, purpose, person or project lineage at the time of choice.

If the original rationale is not independently supported, retain NON-MOOT — naming origin unresolved. An identified human originator is helpful, but the underlying reason still needs evidence.

Independent cultural ancestry, contemporaneous source dates, structure, and actual transmission must be evaluated separately.
